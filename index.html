02 / Start here

What is an OopBuy spreadsheet?

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds, source links, categories and comparison notes. It helps you narrow down options before checking the seller listing and your chosen shopping agent.

This page combines a small CAFANS catalog snapshot with a practical buying guide. It does not take payments, place orders or certify products. Check whether the exact source link is supported in your OopBuy account before making a purchase.

Make every row useful

Save the details that change your decision.

Record the product title, original seller URL, selected size and color, quoted currency, item price and the date checked. Keep unresolved questions beside the listing rather than assuming the missing detail.

For clothing, compare measurements with a garment you already own. For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole length. For any item, confirm what is included and whether a quote covers one piece, a pair or a set.

04 / Look beyond the thumbnail

A practical QC and sizing checklist.

Listing images describe an offer. Warehouse QC, when available, should show the item actually received. Neither alone verifies authenticity or long-term durability.

Questions to resolve before approving an item
CategoryCheck firstUseful follow-up
Shoes & slidesSelected size, pair consistency, sole alignment and visible damage.Request measurements with clear endpoints; compare width as well as length.
Hoodies & T-shirtsChest width, body length, seams, print or embroidery placement.Ask for flat measurements and confirm whether the size chart measures the garment or body.
JacketsShoulder and sleeve measurements, zippers, buttons, lining and supplied parts.Ask for closer images of closures and confirm room for intended layering.
Bags & accessoriesDimensions, hardware, closure function and included straps or components.Confirm material claims separately; photographs cannot establish composition.
Every orderExact variant, quantity, current price and return eligibility.Resolve issues within the applicable deadline before authorizing shipment.

06 / Quick answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ.

Is this the official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. It is not operated by or affiliated with OopBuy. Its catalog references come from CAFANS, and its shopping buttons open CAFANS.

Are these real products from CAFANS?

The 12 titles and displayed USD estimates were taken from the CAFANS featured catalog. This is a static selection, not a live inventory connection. Open CAFANS and search the title to check the current listing.

Can every item be purchased through OopBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for this selection. Obtain the original source link and check that your agent can load the exact product and selected option before paying.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No complete delivered price is provided here. The source describes these as estimated USD product prices. Domestic delivery, international shipping, service charges and any applicable taxes need separate confirmation.

Does a product being listed mean it passed QC?

No. Inclusion is a catalog reference, not a quality endorsement. Review the exact listing and, where available, photos of your own received item. Ask about unclear measurements or visible defects before approving shipment.

What if I cannot find a title on CAFANS?

The source may have changed, renamed or removed the item. Try its distinctive title words or reference number. Treat availability as unconfirmed until you find the matching current listing.

Can I save a comparison spreadsheet?

Use the CSV download above to start a blank comparison sheet. Add the original seller link, selected variant, quoted amounts, checked date and unresolved questions for each candidate.
